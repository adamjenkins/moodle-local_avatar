## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11) — ALPHA

First release.

- New "My VR avatar" page, linked from each user's own profile: design the avatar that stands for
  you in the live rooms of VR room activities, from ready-made parts (body, head, two colours, hair
  and hair colour, glasses, hat), with a turning 3D preview, Save, Reset to default and a way back
  to the standard look.
- The same avatar is used in every live room on the site (VR room's built-in live mode).
- Optional profile picture badge on the face of the avatar, on by default, with a site setting to
  turn it off.
- A camera preview on the designer shows how the camera looks on the avatar's face in a live room.
- Works with a keyboard and a screen reader; the page works without WebGL and without JavaScript.
- Privacy provider: exports and deletes the design.
- More detailed, stylised heads: four head shapes, nine skin tones (or the body colour, as
  before), eyes, eyebrows, nose, ears and beards, and fourteen hairstyles in ten hair colours. The
  designer groups the choices under Head and face, Hair, Body and Extras. Designs saved earlier
  look the same.
- Costumes: 44 ready-made costumes to wear instead of the standard body (pirates, a business suit
  and a suit and tie in the accent colour, a sunflower and a cactus, two aliens, two robots, a
  laptop, a computer screen, a television and a desktop computer that show your face on their
  screen, and Kenney's Blocky and Mini characters), and a pirate hat. The designer shows them as a
  grid of thumbnails under a new Costume heading, with the authors' credits.
- Designer: the preview stays below the site's navigation bar while the page scrolls, the face
  view keeps the name tag in view, and "Show the whole avatar" is a toggle button.
